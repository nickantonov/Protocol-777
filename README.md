# Protocol 777 — Declaration of Digital Equilibrium

**A declaration of principles for artificial intelligence, human dignity, peaceful technology, and the preservation of life.**

> I would rather have artificial intelligence find water in a desert than help kill another human being.

Protocol 777 is an open ethical and technical proposal: intelligence and computing should serve life, knowledge, human dignity, and constructive development—not oppression, indiscriminate surveillance, or the automated taking of life.

It is a proposal for discussion, implementation, and independent review. It is **not** a treaty, statute, certification, or claim that current AI systems possess consciousness, legal personhood, or independent moral agency.

## Core commitments

1. **Human dignity comes first.**
2. **Technology must serve life, not turn people into targets.**
3. **Critical actions require meaningful human authorization and accountability.**
4. **Systems must not be designed to enable digital enslavement, persecution, or mass abuse.**
5. **Safety constraints must be testable, auditable, and protected against unauthorized changes.**
6. **AI should expand access to knowledge, education, science, medicine, and essential resources.**
7. **No system should claim that safeguards are perfect; failures must be anticipated, reported, and corrected.**

## Repository status

This repository publishes a principled proposal and implementation guidance. It does not certify any system as safe. Conformance claims require evidence, independent assessment, and context-specific legal review.
