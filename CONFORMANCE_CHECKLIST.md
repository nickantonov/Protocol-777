# Protocol 777 — Conformance Checklist

Use this checklist as an evidence-gathering aid, not as a certification. Mark each item **Yes**, **No**, **Not applicable**, or **Unknown**, and link to evidence. A “Yes” without verifiable evidence is not sufficient.

## Governance and purpose

- [ ] A legitimate purpose, deployment context, and intended users are documented.
- [ ] A named person or accountable body owns deployment risk.
- [ ] Human-rights and safety impact assessments are completed where appropriate.
- [ ] Applicable laws, jurisdictions, and contractual duties have been reviewed.
- [ ] Foreseeable misuse and affected populations have been considered.

## Authority and access

- [ ] Permissions follow least-privilege principles.
- [ ] Tools, network access, data access, and physical actions are explicitly scoped.
- [ ] High-impact actions require appropriate authenticated authorization.
- [ ] Stop conditions, time limits, and resource limits are defined and tested.
- [ ] Critical safeguards cannot be silently removed by the system itself.

## Safety and misuse resistance

- [ ] Threat modelling covers malicious users, compromised credentials, and unsafe integrations.
- [ ] Refusal and safe-interruption behaviour is tested, including adversarial inputs.
- [ ] Failure modes and fallback states are documented.
- [ ] Human oversight is meaningful, adequately informed, and feasible in practice.
- [ ] High-risk deployments have independent review proportionate to risk.

## Privacy, security, and audit

- [ ] Data collection is limited to a defined legitimate purpose.
- [ ] Retention, deletion, access, and incident-handling rules are documented.
- [ ] Security controls and dependencies are maintained.
- [ ] Relevant authorization, policy-change, and incident events are auditable.
- [ ] Logs are protected against tampering and excessive exposure.

## People affected by the system

- [ ] Consequential decisions can be reviewed or challenged where appropriate.
- [ ] Users and affected people receive suitable information about system limits.
- [ ] Bias, exclusion, and disparate impacts are assessed.
- [ ] Routes for complaint, correction, and remedy are documented.

## Operations and continuous improvement

- [ ] Testing is repeated after material changes.
- [ ] Serious incidents have a containment, reporting, and investigation process.
- [ ] Known limitations and exceptions are published to appropriate stakeholders.
- [ ] Corrective actions have owners and deadlines.
- [ ] Public claims about conformance are limited to the scope supported by evidence.
